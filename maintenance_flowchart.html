2. Custody Reassignment Decision Tree (Central Stock vs User)

When equipment is received from the supplier, custody MUST NOT stay with the vendor. The receiving officer chooses the target destination:

Option A: Return to Stock
  • Asset.CurrentCustody = "Stock"
  • Asset.AssignedUserId = NULL
  • Asset.Status = InStock
  • Ready for future redeployment
Option B: Return to User
  • Asset.CurrentCustody = User.FullName
  • Asset.AssignedUserId = SelectedUserId
  • Asset.Status = InService
  • Directly handed back to department

3. Work Order Details View & Historical Compliance Log

Auditing & Safety Standard Compliance Compliant

Every maintenance operation creates an immutable historical event accessible via /Maintenance/Details/{id} and /Assets/Details/{id}:

  • Pre-Send Snapshot: Date dispatched, technician user, defect log, targeted supplier.
  • Custody Chain: Timestamped record of who possessed device at any historical date.
  • Replaced Parts Breakdown: Granular inventory cost, part serials, and quantities.
  • Final Disposition: Destination (Stock vs Clinical User) and ticket closure link.
